# iOS MIDI Soundbank

`GeneralUser-GS.sf2` is the unmodified GeneralUser GS 2.0.3 bank by S. Christian
Collins. It is used by Apple's system MIDI synthesizer on iOS only.

bank and its packaging. It also describes uncertainty about the origins of some
contained samples; retain the complete accompanying `LICENSE-GeneralUser-GS.txt`.

bundle this directory.
